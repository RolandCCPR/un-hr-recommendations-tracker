# United States of America — Implementation of 2020 (3rd cycle) UPR Recommendations

Assessment of United States of America's implementation of the 347 recommendations received in the 3rd UPR cycle (A/HRC/46/15, 36th session, 2020-11-09), using the Human Rights Committee A–E grading system (CCPR/C/108/2). Evidence is the 4th-cycle pre-sessional documentation (November 2025 (session 50) review).

**Sources**

- **UN Compilation** — A/HRC/WG.6/50/USA/2, OHCHR, 28 August 2025 (UN treaty bodies and special procedures)
- **Stakeholders' Summary** — A/HRC/WG.6/50/USA/3, OHCHR, 1 September 2025 (155 civil-society and NHRI submissions)
- The US **National Report** (A/HRC/WG.6/50/USA/1) was not yet available at the time of assessment.

Grades: **A** largely satisfactory · **B** partially satisfactory · **C** not satisfactory / no relevant action · **D** no information · **E** measures contrary to, or rejection of, the recommendation.

This is a **first-pass assessment for expert review**. Recommendations are grouped by their OHCHR theme; within a theme, near-identical asks share a grade, with separate blocks where the substance (and grade) differ. Each block notes the position United States of America took at the review (supported / noted / supported-noted); a *noted* recommendation was not accepted.

## Summary

| Grade | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| **B** — Partially implemented / steps taken, further action needed | 7 | 2% |
| **C** — Not implemented / no relevant action taken | 219 | 63% |
| **E** — Contrary measures taken, or recommendation rejected | 121 | 35% |
| **Total** | 347 | 100% |

No recommendation met the threshold for **A**. The result is dominated by inaction (**C**) and by 2025 measures running directly counter to the recommendation (**E**).

## Ratification of & accession to international instruments

### Recommendations 26.1-26.22, 26.24, 26.26-26.28, 26.30-26.32, 26.35-26.58, 26.60, 26.61, 26.63 (Ratify outstanding international human rights treaties)

*56 of 347 — position: 39 supported, 11 supported-noted, 6 noted.*

**Grade: C — Information/action not satisfactory**

**Evidence:**
- UN Compilation (A/HRC/WG.6/50/USA/2), para. 2 — Committee on the Elimination of Racial Discrimination: "encouraged the United States of America to consider ratifying the international human rights treaties that it had not yet ratified, including the International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights, the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women, the Convention on the Rights of the Child, ... the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities and the International Convention for the Protection of All Persons from Enforced Disappearance"
- UN Compilation (A/HRC/WG.6/50/USA/2), para. 3 — Human Rights Committee: "recommended that the United States reconsider its position regarding the reservations, declarations and understandings that it had lodged at the time of ratification of the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights"
- Stakeholders' Summary (A/HRC/WG.6/50/USA/3), para. 2 — Several stakeholders (OHCHR summary): "the United States of America (USA) had not yet ratified several international human rights treaties, notwithstanding repeated recommendations to that effect"

**Assessment:** The United States ratified none of the treaties covered by these recommendations in the review period and did not reconsider its ICCPR reservations.

### Recommendations 26.25, 26.29, 26.33, 26.62 (Rescind sanctions on the International Criminal Court)

*4 of 347 — position: 3 supported-noted, 1 noted.*

**Grade: E — Information or measures taken are contrary to, or reflect rejection of, the recommendation**

**Evidence:**
- UN Compilation (A/HRC/WG.6/50/USA/2), para. 25 — Several special procedure mandate holders: "condemned the executive order imposing sanctions on the International Criminal Court ... by sanctioning the Court, the United States weakened the post-Nuremberg commitment to international criminal justice"
- Stakeholders' Summary (A/HRC/WG.6/50/USA/3), para. 6 — Several submissions (OHCHR summary): "Recommendations were made to the USA to ... rescind the Executive Order 14203 sanctioning the ICC and its staff"

**Assessment:** The recommendation was to lift or rescind the sanctions on the International Criminal Court; instead the United States maintained them and in 2025 imposed further sanctions (Executive Order 14203), including against the Court's judges and staff.
