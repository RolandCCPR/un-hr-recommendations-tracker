# Samoa — Implementation of 2021 (3rd cycle) UPR Recommendations

Assessment of Samoa's implementation of the 145 recommendations received in the 3rd UPR cycle (A/HRC/49/7, 39th session, 2021-11-02), using the Human Rights Committee A–E grading system (CCPR/C/108/2). Evidence is the 4th-cycle pre-sessional documentation (November 2026 (session 53) review).

**Sources**

- The **National Report** (A/HRC/WG.6/53/WSM/1) was not yet available at the time of assessment.
- **UN Compilation** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/WSM/2, OHCHR
- **Stakeholders' Summary** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/WSM/3, OHCHR

Grades: **A** largely satisfactory · **B** partially satisfactory · **C** not satisfactory / no relevant action · **D** no information · **E** measures contrary to, or rejection of, the recommendation.

This is a **first-pass assessment for expert review**. Recommendations are grouped by their OHCHR theme; within a theme, near-identical asks share a grade, with separate blocks where the substance (and grade) differ. Each block notes the position Samoa took at the review (supported / noted / supported-noted); a *noted* recommendation was not accepted.

## Summary

| Grade | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| **B** — Partially implemented / steps taken, further action needed | 36 | 25% |
| **C** — Not implemented / no relevant action taken | 87 | 60% |
| **D** — No information available on implementation | 22 | 15% |
| **Total** | 145 | 100% |

**No relevant action (C)** is the dominant outcome, and the evidence is thin on the State's own account: Samoa's National Report was not yet available, so 22 asks have no information in the documents (**D**). **Partial implementation (B)** appears where the documents record a concrete measure — strengthened primary health care and the Health Sector Plan 2021-2025, the Education Sector Plan 2025-2030, higher pensions from late 2025, a legal minimum marriage age of 18 and dedicated policies against gender-based violence. **No relevant action (C)** persists on treaty ratification (ICESCR, ICERD, OP-CAT, the Second Optional Protocol), a comprehensive anti-discrimination law, decriminalisation of same-sex relations, abortion, corporal punishment, the pending Child Protection Bill and the age of criminal responsibility.
