# Hungary — Implementation of 2021 (3rd cycle) UPR Recommendations

Assessment of Hungary's implementation of the 267 recommendations received in the 3rd UPR cycle (A/HRC/49/8, 39th session, 2021-11-02), using the Human Rights Committee A–E grading system (CCPR/C/108/2). Evidence is the 4th-cycle pre-sessional documentation (November 2026 (session 53) review).

**Sources**

- **National Report** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/HUN/1
- **UN Compilation** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/HUN/2, OHCHR
- **Stakeholders' Summary** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/HUN/3, OHCHR

Grades: **A** largely satisfactory · **B** partially satisfactory · **C** not satisfactory / no relevant action · **D** no information · **E** measures contrary to, or rejection of, the recommendation.

This is a **first-pass assessment for expert review**. Recommendations are grouped by their OHCHR theme; within a theme, near-identical asks share a grade, with separate blocks where the substance (and grade) differ. Each block notes the position Hungary took at the review (supported / noted / supported-noted); a *noted* recommendation was not accepted.

## Summary

| Grade | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| **A** — Largely implemented / action largely satisfactory | 4 | 1% |
| **B** — Partially implemented / steps taken, further action needed | 142 | 53% |
| **C** — Not implemented / no relevant action taken | 93 | 35% |
| **D** — No information available on implementation | 12 | 4% |
| **E** — Contrary measures taken, or recommendation rejected | 16 | 6% |
| **Total** | 267 | 100% |

**Partial implementation (B)** is the dominant outcome (142 of 267 recommendations), and Hungary gives a National Report that admits "very limited progress": Act X of 2023 strengthened the National Judicial Council, a preventive restraining order that outlasts imprisonment (2025) and a review of 31,139 police domestic-violence cases, hate-crime training for all police, the National Strategy against Antisemitism (2024), the 2025 action plan under the Social Inclusion Strategy 2030, a partial decriminalisation of defamation (2023) and an Act on anti-discrimination in education (2024). **No relevant action (C)** covers 93 recommendations, notably media pluralism, civil-society space, asylum and pushbacks, the Istanbul Convention and the Women, Peace and Security plan. **Contrary measures (E)** apply to 16 recommendations: the 'child protection' law kept and extended by Act III of 2025, which bans Pride and other LGBTIQ-themed assemblies (a constitutional amendment followed), and the withdrawal from the Rome Statute. Four recommendations reach **A** (support for the family, visible bilingualism in the Rába region, cooperation with special procedures).

### Recommendations 128.1, 128.2, 128.6-128.9, 128.11 (Ratify outstanding international instruments)

*7 of 267 — position: 7 noted.*

**Grade: C — Information/action not satisfactory**

**Assessment:** No ratification is reported; the National Report states only that Hungary is party to most conventions, and UNESCO and stakeholders continue to recommend ratification.
