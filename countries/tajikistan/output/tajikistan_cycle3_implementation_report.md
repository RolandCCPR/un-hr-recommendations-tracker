# Tajikistan — Implementation of 2021 (3rd cycle) UPR Recommendations

Assessment of Tajikistan's implementation of the 234 recommendations received in the 3rd UPR cycle (A/HRC/49/12, 39th session, 2021-11-04), using the Human Rights Committee A–E grading system (CCPR/C/108/2). Evidence is the 4th-cycle pre-sessional documentation (November 2026 (session 53) review).

**Sources**

- **National Report** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/TJK/1
- **UN Compilation** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/TJK/2, OHCHR
- **Stakeholders' Summary** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/TJK/3, OHCHR

Grades: **A** largely satisfactory · **B** partially satisfactory · **C** not satisfactory / no relevant action · **D** no information · **E** measures contrary to, or rejection of, the recommendation.

This is a **first-pass assessment for expert review**. Recommendations are grouped by their OHCHR theme; within a theme, near-identical asks share a grade, with separate blocks where the substance (and grade) differ. Each block notes the position Tajikistan took at the review (supported / noted / supported-noted); a *noted* recommendation was not accepted.

## Summary

| Grade | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| **A** — Largely implemented / action largely satisfactory | 9 | 4% |
| **B** — Partially implemented / steps taken, further action needed | 145 | 62% |
| **C** — Not implemented / no relevant action taken | 56 | 24% |
| **D** — No information available on implementation | 8 | 3% |
| **E** — Contrary measures taken, or recommendation rejected | 16 | 7% |
| **Total** | 234 | 100% |

**Partial implementation (B)** is the dominant outcome (145 of 234 asks), and Tajikistan gives a detailed National Report: the Equality and Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Act (2022, **A**), the National Human Rights Strategy to 2038 (2023, **A**), a National Programme on the Protection of Children's Rights 2026-2030 (**A**), a complete ban on corporal punishment (2024), a new disability-rights Act (June 2026), a 92% budget increase for the Commissioner for Human Rights, amendments to the Domestic Violence Act and a State Programme 2025-2030, trafficking and child-labour plans, and amendments to the Criminal Code on online expression (May 2025). **No relevant action (C)** persists on criminalising domestic violence (still in the draft Criminal Code), the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities and other ratifications, torture investigations, religious freedom, LGBTI protection and a standing invitation to special procedures. **Contrary measures (E)** apply to 16 asks on civil society, media and political prisoners: the UN reports 700 NGOs liquidated in 18 months, about 20 journalists and bloggers imprisoned since 2022 and websites blocked.

## Constitutional & legislative framework

### Recommendations 123.3, 123.46, 123.53, 123.63, 123.75, 123.81, 123.110, 123.154 (Bring legislation into line with international human rights obligations)

*8 of 234 — position: 7 supported, 1 noted.*

**Grade: B — Information/action partially satisfactory**

**Evidence:**
- National Report (A/HRC/WG.6/53/TJK/1), para. 30 — Tajikistan: "On 19 July 2022, the Equality and Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Act was adopted"

**Assessment:** Tajikistan adopted the Equality and Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Act (2022), a new Rights and Social Protection of Persons with Disabilities Act (2026), amended the Domestic Violence Act (2024), aligned the torture definition with the Convention against Torture and adopted new counter-terrorism and counter-extremism acts; the Criminal Code reform remains in draft.
