# Greece — Implementation of 2021 (3rd cycle) UPR Recommendations

Assessment of Greece's implementation of the 239 recommendations received in the 3rd UPR cycle (A/HRC/49/5, 39th session, 2021-11-01), using the Human Rights Committee A–E grading system (CCPR/C/108/2). Evidence is the 4th-cycle pre-sessional documentation (November 2026 (session 53) review).

**Sources**

- **National Report** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/GRC/1
- **UN Compilation** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/GRC/2, OHCHR
- **Stakeholders' Summary** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/GRC/3, OHCHR

Grades: **A** largely satisfactory · **B** partially satisfactory · **C** not satisfactory / no relevant action · **D** no information · **E** measures contrary to, or rejection of, the recommendation.

This is a **first-pass assessment for expert review**. Recommendations are grouped by their OHCHR theme; within a theme, near-identical asks share a grade, with separate blocks where the substance (and grade) differ. Each block notes the position Greece took at the review (supported / noted / supported-noted); a *noted* recommendation was not accepted.

## Summary

| Grade | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| **A** — Largely implemented / action largely satisfactory | 2 | 1% |
| **B** — Partially implemented / steps taken, further action needed | 180 | 75% |
| **C** — Not implemented / no relevant action taken | 37 | 15% |
| **D** — No information available on implementation | 13 | 5% |
| **E** — Contrary measures taken, or recommendation rejected | 7 | 3% |
| **Total** | 239 | 100% |

**Partial implementation (B)** is the dominant outcome, and Greece gives a detailed National Report: marriage equality (Law 5089/2024, **A**), bans on conversion therapy and non-consensual intersex surgery, repeal of simple defamation, a second National Action Plan against Racism and Intolerance (2026), the 2025 EU-aligned domestic-violence law with stalking and cyber-harassment offences, pay-transparency legislation (2026), a National Guardianship System for unaccompanied minors (2024), a National Strategy on Disability 2024-2030, new prison alternatives and a National Climate Law. **No relevant action (C)** persists on treaty ratification (ICRMW, OP-ICESCR, OP-CRC-IC), the rights of the Turkish Muslim minority, the protection of human rights defenders, and investigation of pushbacks. There are clear regressions or rejections (**E**): in July 2025 Greece suspended the registration of asylum applications of people arriving by sea and provided for returns without registration, the Human Rights Committee remains gravely concerned about pushbacks, and Greece restates that it recognises only a religious minority in Thrace.
