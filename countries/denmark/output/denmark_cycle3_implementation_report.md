# Denmark — Implementation of 2021 (3rd cycle) UPR Recommendations

Assessment of Denmark's implementation of the 288 recommendations received in the 3rd UPR cycle (A/HRC/48/10, 38th session, 2021-05-06), using the Human Rights Committee A–E grading system (CCPR/C/108/2). Evidence is the 4th-cycle pre-sessional documentation (7 May 2026 (session 52) review).

**Sources**

- **National Report** — A/HRC/WG.6/52/DNK/1, 20 February 2026
- **UN Compilation** — A/HRC/WG.6/52/DNK/2, OHCHR, 6 February 2026 (UN treaty bodies, UNHCR, UNESCO)
- **Stakeholders' Summary** — A/HRC/WG.6/52/DNK/3, OHCHR, 28 January 2026 (16 submissions + the Danish Institute for Human Rights)

Grades: **A** largely satisfactory · **B** partially satisfactory · **C** not satisfactory / no relevant action · **D** no information · **E** measures contrary to, or rejection of, the recommendation.

This is a **first-pass assessment for expert review**. Recommendations are grouped by their OHCHR theme; within a theme, near-identical asks share a grade, with separate blocks where the substance (and grade) differ. Each block notes the position Denmark took at the review (supported / noted / supported-noted); a *noted* recommendation was not accepted.

## Summary

| Grade | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| **A** — Largely implemented / action largely satisfactory | 10 | 3% |
| **B** — Partially implemented / steps taken, further action needed | 195 | 68% |
| **C** — Not implemented / no relevant action taken | 60 | 21% |
| **D** — No information available on implementation | 8 | 3% |
| **E** — Contrary measures taken, or recommendation rejected | 15 | 5% |
| **Total** | 288 | 100% |

Denmark reports concrete follow-up on many recommendations, so **partial implementation (B)** is the dominant outcome (ratification of the Enforced Disappearance Convention, a consent-based rape law, action plans on racism, trafficking and intimate-partner violence). Persistent gaps remain (no stand-alone torture offence, the retained "Parallel Society" laws) and asylum externalisation is a clear regression (**E**).

## Ratification of & accession to international instruments

### Recommendations 60.1, 60.3-60.7, 60.9, 60.10 (Ratify the Convention against Enforced Disappearance)

*8 of 288 — position: 8 supported.*

**Grade: A — Information/action largely satisfactory**

**Assessment:** Denmark ratified the International Convention for the Protection of All Persons from Enforced Disappearance on 13 January 2022 and submitted its first report to the Committee in May 2025; it has not accepted the individual complaints procedure.

### Recommendations 60.8, 60.13, 60.16-60.20 (Ratify outstanding international instruments)

## Equality & non-discrimination

### Recommendations 60.38-60.50, 60.53, 60.55, 60.56, 60.69, 60.71-60.77, 60.79-60.85, 60.88-60.95, 60.103-60.105, 60.173, 60.191, 60.210, 60.260, 60.283 (Anti-discrimination framework; national action plan against racism)

*47 of 288 — position: 43 supported, 4 noted.*

**Grade: B — Information/action partially satisfactory**

**Evidence:**
- National Report (A/HRC/WG.6/52/DNK/1), para. 112 — Denmark: "In February 2025, the Danish Government presented a national action plan against racism with 36 initiatives and the ambition to fight racism in a variety of areas in society"
- Stakeholders' Summary (A/HRC/WG.6/52/DNK/3), para. 21 — Joint submission 1; Joint submission 2: "welcomed the adoption of a national action plan against racism in February 2025. However, they were concerned that the plan had limitations in scope and lacked a clear definition of racism"

**Assessment:** Denmark has a broad anti-discrimination legal framework and in February 2025 adopted a National Action Plan against Racism with 36 initiatives, alongside successive action plans against antisemitism; CERD and stakeholders nonetheless report persistent gaps - no legislative prohibition of racial or ethnic profiling, weak hate-crime data, and a 2025 plan criticised for limited scope and for lacking a definition of racism.

## Conditions of detention

### Recommendations 60.136, 60.137, 60.140 (Restrict solitary confinement)

*3 of 288 — position: 3 noted.*

**Grade: B — Information/action partially satisfactory**

**Evidence:**
- National Report (A/HRC/WG.6/52/DNK/1), para. 80 — Denmark: "A new disciplinary penalty system entered into force in September 2023, which has led to ... a substantial decrease in long-term solitary confinement exceeding 14 days"
