# Saint Vincent and the Grenadines — Implementation of 2021 (3rd cycle) UPR Recommendations

Assessment of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines's implementation of the 156 recommendations received in the 3rd UPR cycle (A/HRC/49/10, 39th session, 2021-11-03), using the Human Rights Committee A–E grading system (CCPR/C/108/2). Evidence is the 4th-cycle pre-sessional documentation (November 2026 (session 53) review).

**Sources**

- The **National Report** (A/HRC/WG.6/53/VCT/1) was not yet available at the time of assessment.
- **UN Compilation** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/VCT/2, OHCHR
- **Stakeholders' Summary** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/VCT/3, OHCHR

Grades: **A** largely satisfactory · **B** partially satisfactory · **C** not satisfactory / no relevant action · **D** no information · **E** measures contrary to, or rejection of, the recommendation.

This is a **first-pass assessment for expert review**. Recommendations are grouped by their OHCHR theme; within a theme, near-identical asks share a grade, with separate blocks where the substance (and grade) differ. Each block notes the position Saint Vincent and the Grenadines took at the review (supported / noted / supported-noted); a *noted* recommendation was not accepted.

## Summary

| Grade | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| **A** — Largely implemented / action largely satisfactory | 2 | 1% |
| **B** — Partially implemented / steps taken, further action needed | 51 | 33% |
| **C** — Not implemented / no relevant action taken | 79 | 51% |
| **D** — No information available on implementation | 24 | 15% |
| **Total** | 156 | 100% |

**No relevant action (C)** is the dominant outcome, with a thin evidence base on the State's own account because its National Report was not yet available. **Partial implementation (B)** appears where the documents record concrete measures: the 2023 amendment of the Prevention of Trafficking in Persons Act and a 2021-2025 action plan, a National Task Force on Gender-Based Violence (2024), a National Child Protection Policy, the national climate and adaptation framework, expanded social protection after the 2021 volcanic eruption and the new Belle Isle detention facility. **No relevant action (C)** persists on ICPPED, OP-CAT and the other unratified protocols, a comprehensive anti-discrimination law, decriminalisation of same-sex conduct (sections 146 and 148), the death penalty (a de facto moratorium since 1995 but one person on death row), the minimum age of marriage, a national human rights institution, the pending Sexual Offences Bill and criminalisation of marital rape and sexual harassment, and corporal punishment. Many asks have no information in the documents (**D**).
