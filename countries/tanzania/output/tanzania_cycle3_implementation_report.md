# United Republic of Tanzania — Implementation of 2021 (3rd cycle) UPR Recommendations

Assessment of United Republic of Tanzania's implementation of the 252 recommendations received in the 3rd UPR cycle (A/HRC/49/13, 39th session, 2021-11-05), using the Human Rights Committee A–E grading system (CCPR/C/108/2). Evidence is the 4th-cycle pre-sessional documentation (November 2026 (session 53) review).

**Sources**

- **National Report** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/TZA/1
- **UN Compilation** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/TZA/2, OHCHR
- **Stakeholders' Summary** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/TZA/3, OHCHR

Grades: **A** largely satisfactory · **B** partially satisfactory · **C** not satisfactory / no relevant action · **D** no information · **E** measures contrary to, or rejection of, the recommendation.

This is a **first-pass assessment for expert review**. Recommendations are grouped by their OHCHR theme; within a theme, near-identical asks share a grade, with separate blocks where the substance (and grade) differ. Each block notes the position United Republic of Tanzania took at the review (supported / noted / supported-noted); a *noted* recommendation was not accepted.

## Summary

| Grade | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| **A** — Largely implemented / action largely satisfactory | 7 | 3% |
| **B** — Partially implemented / steps taken, further action needed | 143 | 57% |
| **C** — Not implemented / no relevant action taken | 87 | 35% |
| **D** — No information available on implementation | 1 | 0% |
| **E** — Contrary measures taken, or recommendation rejected | 14 | 6% |
| **Total** | 252 | 100% |

**Partial implementation (B)** is the dominant outcome (143 of 252 asks), and Tanzania gives a detailed National Report on socio-economic and service measures: health financing (TZS 1.31 to 3.104 trillion), school construction and a Re-Entry Guideline for pregnant girls, the Second National Plan of Action on violence against women and children (2024/25-2028/29), a National Action Plan on persons with albinism, the Mama Samia legal aid campaign and amendments to the Media Services Act and related laws. **Fully implemented (A)** are the ban on expelling pregnant schoolgirls, birth registration, older persons, climate participation and rural communication infrastructure. **No relevant action (C)** persists on ratifying the Convention against Torture and other treaties, the death penalty (a de facto moratorium only), child marriage (the Law of Marriage Act is still only under consultation), decriminalisation of same-sex relations, corporal punishment and the second National Human Rights Action Plan. **Contrary measures (E)** apply to 14 asks on freedom of expression, assembly, political participation and indigenous peoples: the nationwide internet shutdown after the 29 October 2025 elections, the shutdown of more than 80,000 websites (May 2025), reported lethal force, arrests and enforced disappearances (which the State rejects), disqualification of opposition candidates and the position that no group is indigenous.

## Assessment by theme

## Ratification of & accession to international instruments

### Recommendations 146.1-146.32, 146.38, 146.64 (Ratify outstanding international instruments (CAT, ICPPED, ICRMW and others))

*35 of 252 — position: 30 noted, 5 supported.*

**Grade: C — Information/action not satisfactory**

**Evidence:**
- National Report (A/HRC/WG.6/53/TZA/1), para. 13 — United Republic of Tanzania: "The URT is yet to ratify the international human rights instruments recommended during the 3rd cycle of UPR"
- Stakeholders' Summary (A/HRC/WG.6/53/TZA/3), para. 3 — Stakeholders: "the United Republic of Tanzania had yet to ratify CAT"

**Assessment:** The National Report states that Tanzania is yet to ratify the instruments recommended and continues legal and policy consultations; stakeholders confirm that CAT, ICPPED and others remain unratified.

*1 of 252 — position: 1 noted.*

**Grade: B — Information/action partially satisfactory**
