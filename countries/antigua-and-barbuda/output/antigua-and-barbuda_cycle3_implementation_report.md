# Antigua and Barbuda — Implementation of 2021 (3rd cycle) UPR Recommendations

Assessment of Antigua and Barbuda's implementation of the 155 recommendations received in the 3rd UPR cycle (A/HRC/49/15, 39th session, 2021-11-08), using the Human Rights Committee A–E grading system (CCPR/C/108/2). Evidence is the 4th-cycle pre-sessional documentation (November 2026 (session 53) review).

**Sources**

- **National Report** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/ATG/1
- **UN Compilation** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/ATG/2, OHCHR
- **Stakeholders' Summary** — A/HRC/WG.6/53/ATG/3, OHCHR

Grades: **A** largely satisfactory · **B** partially satisfactory · **C** not satisfactory / no relevant action · **D** no information · **E** measures contrary to, or rejection of, the recommendation.

This is a **first-pass assessment for expert review**. Recommendations are grouped by their OHCHR theme; within a theme, near-identical asks share a grade, with separate blocks where the substance (and grade) differ. Each block notes the position Antigua and Barbuda took at the review (supported / noted / supported-noted); a *noted* recommendation was not accepted.

## Summary

| Grade | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| **A** — Largely implemented / action largely satisfactory | 9 | 6% |
| **B** — Partially implemented / steps taken, further action needed | 68 | 44% |
| **C** — Not implemented / no relevant action taken | 59 | 38% |
| **D** — No information available on implementation | 19 | 12% |
| **Total** | 155 | 100% |

**Partial implementation (B)** is the dominant outcome, and Antigua and Barbuda submitted a detailed National Report: the High Court decriminalised consensual same-sex relations in July 2022 (**A**, though the provisions were not formally repealed), ILO Conventions 190 and 183 were ratified in 2022, maternity leave rose to 14 weeks, a National Child Protection Policy (2021), a Sexual Offenders Registry (2022), a GBV Action Plan (2023), a shelter partnership, prison upgrades, higher pensions (2025-2026) and large climate-resilience financing were reported. **No relevant action (C)** persists on the unratified treaties and protocols (ICPPED, OP-CAT, the Second Optional Protocol), a national human rights institution, a national reporting mechanism, anti-discrimination legislation, criminalisation of marital rape, abortion, corporal punishment in schools and formal abolition of the death penalty (a de facto moratorium since 1991). No trafficking conviction has ever been secured.
