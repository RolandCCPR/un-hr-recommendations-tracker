# Paraguay — Implementation of 2021 (3rd cycle) UPR Recommendations

Assessment of Paraguay's implementation of the 210 recommendations received in the 3rd UPR cycle (A/HRC/48/9, 38th session, 2021-05-04), using the Human Rights Committee A–E grading system (CCPR/C/108/2). Evidence is the 4th-cycle pre-sessional documentation (6 May 2026 (session 52) review).

**Sources**

- **National Report** — A/HRC/WG.6/52/PRY/1
- **UN Compilation** — A/HRC/WG.6/52/PRY/2, OHCHR
- **Stakeholders' Summary** — A/HRC/WG.6/52/PRY/3, OHCHR

Grades: **A** largely satisfactory · **B** partially satisfactory · **C** not satisfactory / no relevant action · **D** no information · **E** measures contrary to, or rejection of, the recommendation.

This is a **first-pass assessment for expert review**. Recommendations are grouped by their OHCHR theme; within a theme, near-identical asks share a grade, with separate blocks where the substance (and grade) differ. Each block notes the position Paraguay took at the review (supported / noted / supported-noted); a *noted* recommendation was not accepted.

## Summary

| Grade | Count | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| **A** — Largely implemented / action largely satisfactory | 1 | 0% |
| **B** — Partially implemented / steps taken, further action needed | 124 | 59% |
| **C** — Not implemented / no relevant action taken | 76 | 36% |
| **D** — No information available on implementation | 5 | 2% |
| **E** — Contrary measures taken, or recommendation rejected | 4 | 2% |
| **Total** | 210 | 100% |

**Partial implementation (B)** is the dominant outcome: Paraguay reports a concrete on-point measure in the review period across most themes (the SIMORE Plus follow-up system, monetary poverty down from ~26% to ~20%, a universal old-age pension, Inter-American Court land-restitution compliance with 637 Indigenous consultations, judicial digitalisation, 100% school-meals coverage, a new women's prison plus a 2024 alternatives-to-detention protocol, 2025 treaty ratifications, the Indigenous Languages Act, disability-certification reform, online birth registration). **No relevant action (C)** persists on the long-standing asks — a comprehensive anti-discrimination law, a migration law, abortion, criadazgo, the minimum marriage age, the journalist-protection bill — and there are clear regressions (**E**): the 2024 NGO "Garrote" law and Ministry-of-Education restrictions on gender and sexuality education.

### Recommendations 118.6-118.11 (Ratify outstanding international instruments)

*6 of 210 — position: 6 supported.*

**Grade: B — Information/action partially satisfactory**

## Equality & non-discrimination

### Recommendations 118.30, 118.31, 118.36, 118.38-118.47 (Protect LGBTIQ+ persons from discrimination)

*13 of 210 — position: 13 supported.*

**Grade: C — Information/action not satisfactory**

**Evidence:**
- UN Compilation (A/HRC/WG.6/52/PRY/2), para. 21 — Amnesty International: "impunity for hate speech and the promotion of discrimination against lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender and intersex persons"
- Stakeholders' Summary (A/HRC/WG.6/52/PRY/3), para. 61 — REPAR+: "the situation of the LGBTQ+ population in Paraguay remained marked by the absence of legislation, by institutional and social discrimination, and the rise of hate speech"

**Assessment:** There is no anti-discrimination legislation protecting LGBTIQ+ people, no legal recognition of same-sex civil unions or of self-perceived gender identity, and stakeholders and Amnesty International report rising hate speech and violence with impunity.

## Conditions of detention

### Recommendations 118.53, 118.55, 118.56, 118.58, 118.60-118.62, 118.66, 118.70, 118.71 (Prison overcrowding; pre-trial detention; torture prevention)

*10 of 210 — position: 10 supported.*

**Grade: B — Information/action partially satisfactory**

**Evidence:**
- UN Compilation (A/HRC/WG.6/52/PRY/2), para. 17 — United Nations country team: "A total of 70% of the prison population was in pretrial detention, and overcrowding had reached 435% nationwide, with some facilities operating at more than 1,100% of their capacity"
- National Report (A/HRC/WG.6/52/PRY/1), para. 64 — Paraguay: "A historic milestone was achieved with the definitive closure of the Casa del Buen Pastor Prison and the opening of a women's prison complex in Emboscada with capacity for more than 1,200 women"
- Stakeholders' Summary (A/HRC/WG.6/52/PRY/3), para. 25 — National Mechanism for the Prevention of Torture of Paraguay: "the persistent abuse of pretrial detention; overcrowding; maximum security regimes that constitute torture; corruption and control of prisons by criminal groups"

**Assessment:** Paraguay closed the Casa del Buen Pastor prison, opened a 1,200-place women's prison in Emboscada and approved a 2024 protocol on alternatives to pre-trial detention, but the UN country team and the National Mechanism for the Prevention of Torture report a structural crisis: 70 per cent of prisoners in pre-trial detention, 435 per cent overcrowding nationwide (over 1,100 per cent in some units), and maximum-security regimes amounting to torture.

### Recommendations 118.99-118.107, 118.207 (Combat trafficking in persons)

*10 of 210 — position: 10 supported.*

**Grade: B — Information/action partially satisfactory**

**Evidence:**

- UN Compilation (A/HRC/WG.6/52/PRY/2), para. 26 — Committee on Migrant Workers: "Paraguay had not yet achieved satisfactory standards in its fight against trafficking in persons and that children, especially those living on the street in the tri-border area, continued to be subjected to exploitation"
