How countries follow through on what they are told to do — two UN mechanisms, graded A to E. Data generated —.

Two UN mechanisms produce concrete recommendations to States and later judge how far each was carried out: the Universal Periodic Review (peer review of every country) and the Human Rights Committee (the ICCPR treaty body). This site consolidates both, on the Committee's own A–E scale, for the countries assessed so far.
